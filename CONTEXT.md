# Sales Intelligence Agent

A Gmail and Google Calendar copilot that helps small service companies handle incoming customer appointment requests.

## Language

**Scheduling request**:
An incoming customer email asking to arrange an appointment.
_Avoid_: Lead, ticket

**Scheduling copilot**:
The product that proposes available times, drafts a reply, and prepares a calendar event for approval.
_Avoid_: AI employee, autonomous agent

**Operations lead**:
The person responsible for the shared inbox and appointment calendar at the customer company.
_Avoid_: Admin, user

**Appointment calendar**:
The dedicated Google Calendar whose full event details the scheduling copilot automatically reads when an event is created or updated.
_Avoid_: Company calendar, personal calendar

**Approval**:
The operations lead's single explicit confirmation that sends the reviewed reply and creates its selected appointment-calendar event.
_Avoid_: Automation, auto-send

**User test**:
A guided trial with a prospective operations lead using synthetic scheduling requests and calendars rather than their production Google data.
_Avoid_: Pilot, deployment
